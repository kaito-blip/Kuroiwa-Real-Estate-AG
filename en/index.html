Ethos

Kuroiwa — 黒岩 — means “black rock”. The name is a statement of intent: a rock does not move with the mood of the market. It stands firm while others waver, and it is there long before anyone notices it. That is how we work.

The most interesting properties in Switzerland are never listed. They change hands in conversations built on trust — between people who know each other, and through intermediaries who keep their word. This is the market we call home. We pick up the phone before others publish. We examine before others promise. And we close while others are still printing brochures.

Investing our own capital changes every conversation: we know both sides of the table — the seller’s impatience and the buyer’s caution. Those who entrust us with a property do not get a broker who mediates. They get a partner who understands.

“Discretion is not a courtesy.
It is our business model.”
Kaito Weingart, Owner
